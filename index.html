Quiénes somosWho we are

Maguaré es una reserva indígena cerca de Leticia, sobre el río Amazonas. Aquí no encontrarás una cadena ni un edificio: encontrarás a una comunidad que abre su territorio para compartir su forma de vivir con quienes llegan con respeto. Lo que pagas sostiene a las familias y al cuidado del bosque. Maguaré is an Indigenous reserve near Leticia, on the Amazon river. You won't find a chain or a building here: you'll find a community that opens its territory to share its way of life with those who arrive with respect. What you pay sustains the families and the care of the forest.
